Surabaya, The City of Heroes.

Surabaya is the capital of East Java and the second-largest city in Indonesia. It is deeply associated with the historic Battle of November 10, 1945, which earned it the nickname “The City of Heroes.”

Its symbol features a shark and a crocodile, which are believed to be the origins of the city’s name. Surabayans are known for being straightforward, warm-hearted, and passionate about good food.
