Flavors That Feel Like Home

Rawon

A rich beef soup with a deep, dark broth made from keluak. Savory and packed with spices, it is best enjoyed with salted egg, bean sprouts, and sambal.

Lontong Balap

Rice cakes, fried tofu, bean sprouts, and lentho served with warm garlic broth, accompanied by cockle satay and savory shrimp paste sauce.

Rujak Cingur

Vegetables, fruits, and beef snout tossed in a rich shrimp paste sauce. Sweet, spicy, and savory flavors come together on one plate.
