Discover the places that make this city unforgettable.

Surabaya Bridge

A beautiful coastal bridge in Surabaya, known for its unique curved design and dancing fountain. Enjoy the sea breeze and the beautiful views of Kenjeran.

Submarine Monument

KRI Pasopati, a former submarine that has been transformed into a museum. Visitors can step inside and explore its interior spaces.

Tunjungan Street

One of Surabaya’s most iconic streets, filled with history, food, shops, and lively city vibes. Take a walk, enjoy the atmosphere, and experience the heart of Surabaya.
